Memoiro

Keep the life story of your parents and grandparents, in their own words and voice, and turn it into a book you can print.

Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Write to jonas.volk89@gmail.com – we usually answer within two working days.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Memoiro Pro can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Moving to a new iPhone: in Memoiro, open Settings → "Save a backup" and keep the file, for example in iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, tap "Restore from a backup" and choose the file. iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) also include Memoiro.

Answers by voice message

Send a question from Memoiro through WhatsApp, Messages or any other app. When the answer arrives as a voice message, tap and hold it, choose Share (or Forward → Share) and pick Memoiro. If Memoiro is not listed, save the voice message to the Files app and import it from the story in Memoiro. The recording is written down on your iPhone; the text can be corrected at any time.

Printing the book

Memoiro creates a print-ready PDF in A5 or 6 × 9 inches. You can print it at home or in a copy shop, or upload it to a book printing service that accepts your own PDF. Memoiro has no partnership with any printing service.

Recording other people

Please only record people who agree to it. Recording a private conversation without permission is not allowed in many countries. Memoiro asks you to confirm consent before the first recording of each person.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Memoiro ("the app") is developed by Jonas Volk, Switzerland. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

No data collection

Memoiro does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Data stored on your device

Everything you add – names, portraits, questions, recordings, transcripts, texts and photos – is stored only on your device. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, this data may be included in your backup under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete a person, a story or all data in the app; deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Speech recognition

Recordings are written down with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is not sent to us or to Apple's servers for this. On iOS 26 and later, iOS may download a speech model for the language from Apple once; this download contains no personal data.

Sharing and exports

Questions you send, the book PDF, audio exports and backup files are created on your device. They only leave it if you choose to share them, and then only to the recipient or place you pick.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the weekly reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple shares only anonymous, aggregated sales information with developers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
